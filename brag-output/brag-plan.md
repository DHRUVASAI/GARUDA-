## 1. Planning Rubric

1. **What is it?**  
   GARUDA is an autonomous personal network guardian, live Scapy packet IDS, ML behavioral anomaly detector, and Ed25519 Merkle forensic ledger that protects homes, home-labs, and remote developer setups from stealth network attacks.

2. **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?**  
   It is for developers, engineers, and everyday users who have smart home IoT devices, laptops, and phones connected to a blind Wi-Fi router. It turns unmonitored home networks into zero-trust defensive perimeters without enterprise complexity.

3. **What sets it apart? (The Unfair Advantage)**  
   - 5-layer multi-protocol real device naming (zero "Unknown Device" entries).  
   - Raw L2-L7 Scapy packet sniffing catching ARP poisoning, SYN floods, and stealth scans.  
   - 8D Isolation Forest ML tensor scoring detecting covert C2 beacons.  
   - RFC 6962 SHA-256 Merkle Forensic Ledger sealing immutable audit trails with Ed25519 signatures.  
   - Single-command live deployment (`garuda`) and AWS Hybrid readiness (KMS, S3 Attestation Anchor, SQS alert pipeline).

4. **What is the most impressive claim?**  
   "Enterprise SIEMs cost $100,000/year. GARUDA delivers real-time packet IDS, ML behavioral anomaly detection, and tamper-proof cryptographic proofs right on your laptop in 1 command."

5. **What is the visual hook?**  
   A high-energy cyber-radar with red threat blips expanding into a multi-ring tactical topology where alerts, unverified devices, and safe nodes never overlap.

6. **What real UI and walkthrough flows are shown?**  
   - Tactical 2D Radar with AABB collision-free label engine.  
   - 3D WebGL PBR Topology with 1,000+ device instancing.  
   - Real-time Threat Drawer and 1-Click OS Firewall Isolation.  
   - Cryptographic Ledger Attestation blocks with Merkle inclusion proofs.  
   - Safe Simulation Lab red-team attack injection.

7. **Tone:**  
   `cinematic` meets `polished` — high-octane aerospace/defense aesthetic, razor-sharp typography, real technical telemetry, and unapologetic engineering craft.
