## Planning Rubric

1. **What is it?**
   GARUDA v4 is an open-source, autonomous personal network guardian that turns your laptop into a real-time packet IDS, behavioral ML anomaly detector, Ed25519 Merkle attestation ledger, and host firewall controller.

2. **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?**
   It's for developers, security engineers, homelabbers, and everyday users who have dozens of smart appliances and devices on their home Wi-Fi but zero intrusion visibility from basic ISP routers.

3. **What sets it apart?**
   Unlike DNS sinkholes (Pi-hole), it catches Layer 2 ARP poisoning, rogue DHCP servers, and direct IP C2 beaconing. Unlike enterprise SIEMs (CrowdStrike/Splunk), it is 100% self-hosted, lightweight, runs locally without cloud telemetry, and features an interactive 3D spatial radar.

   "Your router sees zero threats. Enterprise SIEMs cost \$100,000/year. GARUDA gives you 5-layer hardware device naming, sub-11ms anomaly detection, and court-admissible cryptographic Merkle proofs right on your workstation."

   - 5-layer real device identity resolution (Windows 11 Laptop, LG Smart TV, Zyxel Fiber Router, Canon Printer).

7. **Tone:**
   `cinematic` / `polished` — sleek cybersecurity command center, neon cyan and emerald on obsidian slate, high-energy 120 BPM electronic soundscape, crisp typography, and punchy cuts on the beat.
